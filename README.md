# Luca Faccio

**Product Designer with a background in web development.**
Based in Amsterdam.

I design digital products by connecting how a system works with how
people need to use it. My focus is on platforms and tools where different
roles, workflows and business rules need to become a clear, usable
experience.

## How I approach product design

I start by understanding what people are trying to do, the rules they
work within, and where the process becomes difficult. From there, I
shape the structure and behaviour of the product, including what
happens beyond the ideal path.

I use prototypes to make decisions tangible and documentation to
explain the reasoning behind them. The aim is to give design and
engineering a shared understanding of what the product should do.

Design systems are part of this work. I care about what should be
shared, what belongs to an individual product, and how patterns
behave across different contexts.

My development background helps me assess technical constraints,
build working prototypes and stay involved as designs become
software. I use code to explore and test ideas, with the product
experience guiding the decisions.
